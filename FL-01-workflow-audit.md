# FL-01 — AI Workflow Audit

## My current workflow

I looked at the things I regularly spend time on during a normal week and
thought about where AI would actually be useful instead of assuming it
should be involved in everything.

| Task | AI approach | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Applying for internships | Delegate to AI with review | AI can help me find suitable roles, improve my resume and draft applications, but I need to check everything before submitting it. |
| Writing/improving my resume | Collaborate with AI | I know my own experience best, while AI can help with wording, structure and making it clearer. |
| College assignments | Delegate to AI with review | AI can help me understand topics, organise answers and check my work, but I still need to understand and verify what I submit. |
| Studying for exams | Collaborate with AI | I can use AI to explain difficult topics, create practice questions and test my understanding. |
| Going to the gym | Just me | The important part is actually showing up and doing the workout. AI can help with planning, but it cannot replace the activity itself. |
| Planning my diet | Collaborate with AI | AI can help organise meals and estimate nutrition, but I need to decide what actually works with my routine and preferences. |
| Playing cricket | Just me | Improving at cricket requires actual practice and playing rather than just getting information from AI. |
| Playing badminton | Just me | AI can suggest drills or techniques, but the actual practice has to come from me. |
| Playing chess | Collaborate with AI | I can use AI to analyse games, understand mistakes and practise, while still doing the thinking and playing myself. |
| Learning about stocks | Delegate to AI with review | AI can help explain companies and financial concepts and organise research, but important information needs to be checked before I rely on it. |
| Researching topics for personal projects | Delegate to AI with review | AI can speed up the initial research, but I should verify important facts and sources. |
| Organising my weekly tasks | Delegate to AI with review | AI can turn a list of things I need to do into a schedule, but I decide what I actually want to prioritise. |

## What I learned from the audit

Looking at my normal week, I don't think AI should be involved in everything.
It makes the most sense for things like studying, internship applications,
research and planning, where it can save time or help me think through
something.

There are also tasks where AI is not really the point. Going to the gym,
playing cricket and playing badminton are good examples. AI can give me
advice, but the actual activity and improvement still have to come from me.

For most of the tasks in between, I think the best approach is to work with
AI while still checking the result myself. This is especially important for
things like college work, internship applications and financial research.

## My three focus tasks

I chose these three tasks to focus on in the following AI Fluency
assignments.

### 1. Internship applications

**Goal:**  
Use AI to help me find suitable internship opportunities and improve my
application materials without blindly submitting AI-generated content.

**What "done well" means:**

- The internship matches my skills and interests.
- My resume is appropriately tailored to the role.
- The application still sounds like me.
- I personally review everything before submitting it.
- AI does not invent experience, skills or achievements.

### 2. Studying

**Goal:**  
Use AI as a study partner to understand difficult topics and test whether I
actually understand them.

**What "done well" means:**

- I can explain the topic in my own words afterwards.
- AI helps me learn rather than simply giving me answers to copy.
- Important technical information is checked against my course material.
- I finish with useful notes, questions or practice problems.

### 3. Stock research

**Goal:**  
Use AI to make stock research easier by helping me understand companies,
financial concepts and organise information, while keeping the final
decision with me.

**What "done well" means:**

- Important facts and numbers are checked against reliable sources.
- AI clearly separates facts from interpretation.
- I understand the reasons for and against researching a company further.
- The output does not simply tell me to buy or sell something.
- I make the final decision myself.

## Final reflection

The main thing I took from this exercise is that using AI effectively is not
just about knowing how to prompt it. I first need to decide whether AI should
be involved at all, then explain what I actually need, check whether the
result is useful, and take responsibility for how I use it.

For me, the biggest opportunities are repetitive work and learning tasks,
especially internship applications, studying and research. At the same time,
I want to keep the parts that actually require my own judgement, effort and
responsibility.
